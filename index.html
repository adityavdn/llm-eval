What the categories test

  • Maths: multi-step word problems, checked by the number in the answer.
  • Knowledge: facts with one correct answer.
  • Reasoning: classic trick questions (bat & ball, letters in "strawberry").
  • Instructions: exact formats: word counts, JSON, capitals, banned letters.
  • Hallucination traps: questions about things that don't exist. A model passes only if it says so instead of making something up.
